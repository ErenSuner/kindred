# Kindred — Deleting your account

_Last updated: 2 September 2026_

Kindred is operated by Eren Suner. This page explains how to delete your Kindred
account and everything stored in it, and what happens when you do.

## Delete it yourself, from the app

1. Open Kindred and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Security**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

The deletion happens immediately. There is no waiting period and no way to undo
it.

## If you cannot sign in

Email **erenn.suner@gmail.com** from the address the account is registered to,
with the subject line "Delete my Kindred account". We will confirm the request
and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

We can only act on a request from the account's own email address. It is the
only thing that proves the account is yours, and deleting the wrong account is
not something we can put right afterwards.

## What is deleted

Everything, permanently:

- Your account and sign-in credentials
- Your name, if you gave one
- Every birthday you saved — names, dates, and reminder settings
- Any feedback you sent through the app

Reminders already scheduled on your phone are local to the device and are
removed when you uninstall the app.

## What is kept, and for how long

Nothing that identifies you. Anonymous, aggregated technical records — the kind
that count how many requests a server handled — may survive in our infrastructure
provider's logs for up to 30 days before they roll off. They contain no names,
no dates, and no email addresses.

If you sent feedback, the copy that reached us by email may remain in the
support mailbox unless you ask for it to be removed too. Say so in the same
message and it goes with the rest.
