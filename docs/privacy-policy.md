# Kindred — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 September 2026_

Kindred ("the app") remembers birthdays for you: you save a name and a date, and
the app reminds you before the day arrives. This policy explains what the app
collects, why, and the choices you have. Kindred is a personal app for a single
account; there is no sharing, no social layer, and no selling of data.

## Who is responsible

Kindred is operated by Eren Suner. For any privacy question or request, contact
**erenn.suner@gmail.com**.

## What we collect

We only collect what you enter:

- **Account data** — your email address and, optionally, your name and surname.
  Used to sign you in and label your account.
- **Birthdays** — for each one, a name, a date, and how much warning you want
  before it. The year is optional; without it no age is recorded.
- **Feedback you send** — if you use the feedback form, the message you write,
  an optional reply address, and the app version and platform you sent it from.

We do **not** collect location, advertising identifiers, browsing history,
contacts, photos, or analytics about how you tap around the app.

## Device permissions

- **Notifications** — to deliver the birthday reminders you set up. Reminders
  are scheduled locally on your device; the app does not use push servers.
  Optional.

That is the only permission the app asks for. Denying it disables reminders and
nothing else.

## Where your data lives

Your data is stored in our backend provider, **Supabase**, which hosts the
database. Access is protected by row-level security so that each account can
reach only its own data. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections
(HTTPS/TLS). The app uploads no files.

## Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is
processed only by the infrastructure providers needed to run the app (Supabase
for storage; the app store and, if enabled, Sentry for crash diagnostics).
Crash reports contain technical error information, not the names and dates you
have saved.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from **Settings → Security → Delete
account**. This permanently removes your account and every birthday saved in it
from our backend. The action cannot be undone.

If you cannot sign in, [Deleting your account](delete-account.html) explains how
to request deletion by email and exactly what is removed.

## Children

Kindred is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
data from them.

## Changes

after an update means you accept the revised policy.
